Technical Score by Sentiment Regime ▼

Same technical_score-only walk-forward test as the backtest above, but every prediction is tagged with that day's actual sentiment reading, then split by whether it was above or below the median. If technical_score performs meaningfully differently between the two halves, that's evidence the right fix isn't a fixed weight — it's switching which feature leads depending on current sentiment regime.
